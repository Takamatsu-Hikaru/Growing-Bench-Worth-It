We analyze RouteModel 2.3.1 (artifact RM-2.3.1, route configuration v7) on the fixed 600-item English test split of ErrorBench-English v1.0. The frozen system uses deterministic greedy routing, temperature zero, and the benchmark's exact route-match scorer; 506 items are correct and 94 are errors. The system artifact, configuration identifier, gold route, prediction, and exact-match outcome are recorded for every analyzed error. Before labeling, on 2026-07-01 we froze a five-category taxonomy and written instructions in the supplied taxonomy record. Route omission means that a required route was absent; label ambiguity means that exact-match scoring marks a documented competing interpretation wrong; entity extraction means that a missed entity changed the route; unsupported format means that the input was outside the frozen parser contract; other is residual. Two annotators, blinded to one another's labels, independently labeled all 94 errors and then adjudicated disagreements without changing the taxonomy. We report raw agreement and Cohen's kappa over the 94 single-label decisions. The package includes all 29 benchmark ambiguity notes referenced by the label-ambiguity records. We make no claim about a customer- or template-level sampling population; the analysis is a census of the 94 errors in this fixed test set.
